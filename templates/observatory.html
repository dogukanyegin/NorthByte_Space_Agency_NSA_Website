The NSA Astronomical Observatory is a proposed programme for astronomical observation, remote telescope integration and educational research.

○ CONCEPT STAGE — NO PHYSICAL FACILITY CLAIMED
THE CONCEPT

An observatory
built in phases.

Begin with accessible astronomical datasets, observation planning, public research logs and software. Develop toward practical telescope acquisition or hosted remote telescope access, where viable.

Any dedicated physical observation site will require equipment, location evaluation, operating procedures and applicable permissions.

DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
