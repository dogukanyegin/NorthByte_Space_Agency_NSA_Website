NSA / DATA PROTECTION

Privacy notice.

What information the NSA website processes and why.

Who operates this site?

NorthByte Space Agency (NSA) is an internal division of NorthByte Norway Tec Ltd. The company operates the website and manages messages received through the contact form.

Contact form data

We collect your name, email address, subject and message when you choose to contact us. These details are stored in a protected website database and used to review and respond to your inquiry. Sending a message does not create an account or newsletter subscription.

Retention and deletion

Messages remain stored until they are removed by an administrator. We aim to retain only information needed for inquiries and administrative records. To request access, correction or deletion of your submitted information, use the contact form and clearly describe the request.

Cookies

The site uses a signed session cookie for administrator logins and form security (CSRF protection). The public website does not install advertising or analytics cookies in this version.

Third-party map and data services

Earth observation pages load map and satellite imagery assets from external providers including NASA GIBS and the Leaflet CDN; the satellite tracker and events panels request source-derived information via our server. External providers may receive routine connection information such as IP addresses when their resources are loaded. Their own privacy terms apply.

Data security

The site includes access controls for the administrative inbox. Production deployment additionally requires HTTPS, strong administrator credentials, backups and properly restricted server access.

Changes

This privacy notice may be updated when services change. Always consult the current notice and contact NSA for questions about information handling.
