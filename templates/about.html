NSA / THE ORGANISATION

Built for curiosity.
Grounded in evidence.

NorthByte Space Agency is an internal space research and technology division of NorthByte Norway Tec Ltd., pursuing computational tools for understanding Earth and space.

WHO WE ARE / 01

Research as a
practical discipline.

NSA focuses on orbital data exploration, Earth observation, astronomy and scientific software. We aim to develop tools and publish technical work that can be independently inspected and improved.

Our first programmes use established, attributed public data providers. Where an activity is only proposed, it is labelled accordingly.

LEGAL / OPERATIONS

NorthByte Norway Tec Ltd.

NSA operates as an internal division of the existing UK-registered company. It is not a separately incorporated entity or a governmental space agency.

SUPPORT

House of Yegin

House of Yegin supports NSA as a patron/supporter. It is not the owner, parent company or governing body of NSA.

RESEARCH ETHOS

Independent and transparent

We distinguish validated data, computed estimates and aspirational programmes. Source acknowledgements accompany public tools.
